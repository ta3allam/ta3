## 📈 XP Progression & Level Thresholds

- **Level 1 (Junior Agent)**: `0 – 299 XP`

- **Level 3 (Senior Systems Architect)**: `700+ XP`

- `+150 XP`: Delivering a major feature (Database Schema, Storage Buckets, Realtime Engine, Auth JS SDK, CQRS Engine).
- `+100 XP`: Performing a security/QA/architecture audit or DoD sign-off.
- `+50 XP`: Resolving merge conflicts or bug fixes.
